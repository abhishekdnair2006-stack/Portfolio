How I work

⚙

Think in systems. Ship in steps. Good software rarely arrives in one leap — I prefer a visible, testable process where each decision earns its place.

01

Frame

Understand the real problem, its users, constraints, and the outcome worth building toward.

02

Model

Map the data, states, and core logic before polishing the surface layer.

03

Build

Turn the smallest complete path into working software, then test it in context.

04

Refine

Reduce friction, strengthen feedback, and keep the system maintainable over time.
